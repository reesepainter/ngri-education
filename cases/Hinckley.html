Case Details:

John Hinckley Jr. attempted to assassinate U.S. President Ronald Reagan in Washington, D.C., wounding the President, press secretary James Brady, a police officer, and a Secret Service agent.

Verdict:

A jury found Hinckley not guilty by reason of insanity after defense experts showed he suffered from severe psychotic delusions and erotomania regarding actress Jodie Foster.
