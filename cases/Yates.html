Case Details:

In 2001, Andrea Yates drowned her five young children in the bathtub of their Houston, Texas, home.

Verdict:

Following a overturned initial conviction due to flawed expert testimony, her 2006 retrial resulted in a verdict of not guilty by reason of insanity. Although some see this defense as a loophole she is still committed to a state mental hospital since the 2006 retrial. This case again used the M'naghten rule to help prove that Andrea Yates was no in the right state of mind when drowning her children.
