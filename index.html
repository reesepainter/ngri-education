Legal Defintion

Legal defintion of Not Guilty by Reason of Insanity: Not guilty by reason of insanity (NGRI) is a legal defense and verdict where a defendant is absolved of criminal responsibility because a severe mental disease or defect prevented them from understanding the nature of their actions or telling right from wrong at the time of the offense

Simplified Defintion

In layman terms this means: this is a defense that defendants can use in the court of law to help prove they are not guilty of a crime based on a mental disorder/disease that can be upheld by certain professions(pyschologists,etc)
